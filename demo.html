System behaviour

  • When the beam is interrupted the buzzer and primary LED begin a 10s alert cycle.
  • Alert blink-rate increases in the final 5 seconds to draw attention.
  • Secondary LED remains on for 45 seconds as a persistent status indicator.

How to test safely

  1. Power the kit from a 5V USB adapter or 9V adapter as required.
  2. Mount emitter and receiver on stable surfaces and align the beam.
  3. Walk through the beam to verify alert sequence and status LED behavior.
