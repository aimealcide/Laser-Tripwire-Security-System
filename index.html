Protect What Matters Most

Our Laser Tripwire Security System detects beam interruptions with millisecond precision and triggers both audible and visual alerts. Designed for doorways, corridors, display cases, server racks and other sensitive areas.

Integrated camera monitoring: when the laser beam is broken the system captures a photo and securely sends it to the owner's device (phone or email), providing instant visual verification alongside the audible/visual alerts.
