Key features

🔒

Precision detection

Fast optical sensor with configurable sensitivity to reduce false alarms.

🔊

Audible & visual alerts

Programmable buzzer patterns and bright LED alarm for immediate notification.

📸

Integrated camera monitoring

On-beam-break the camera captures a photo (or short clip) and securely sends it to the owner's device for visual verification.

⚙️

Adjustable timing

10s alert cycle by default; timing and blink rates can be customized in firmware.

🔋

Flexible power

Operates from 5V to 12V DC — supports USB power banks or small adapters.

🧰

Mounting & alignment

Aluminum adjustable mounts included for precise aim and stable installation.

🧾

Documentation & support

Quick-start guide, wiring diagrams, and email support included with every kit.
